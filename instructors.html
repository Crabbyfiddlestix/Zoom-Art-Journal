Mahesh & Maitri · Zoom Art School

Two people. One shared love of art.

Mahesh and Maitri are the Founder, Co-Founder and instructors behind Zoom Art School.

The Story

Why
Zoom
Art School?

Zoom Art School was created around a simple idea: learning art should be enjoyable, encouraging and accessible.

Rather than treating art as something that has to be perfect, the school creates room for students to explore, ask questions, practise and discover their own creativity.

That philosophy runs through the classes, the student work and the community that Mahesh and Maitri have built.
