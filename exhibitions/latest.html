The Exhibition

Every piece
has a story.

An exhibition isn't simply a collection of finished drawings. It's a record of learning. Every line, colour, composition and creative decision represents a student trying something, learning something or discovering something new.

This exhibition brings those moments together in one place — celebrating the students of Zoom Art School and the work they have created.
